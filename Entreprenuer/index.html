Entrepreneurship drives innovation, employment, and economic growth.

What is Entrepreneurship?

Entrepreneurship is the process of starting and managing a business by taking risks and using innovative ideas to create value and solve problems.

Importance of Entrepreneurship

Employment

Creates job opportunities and reduces unemployment.

Innovation

Introduces new products, services, and technologies.

Economic Growth

Boosts national income and improves living standards.

Entrepreneurial Skills

  • Leadership
  • Creativity
  • Risk Management
  • Decision Making
  • Communication
